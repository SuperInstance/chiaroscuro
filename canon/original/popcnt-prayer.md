source: AUTHOR
date: 2026-10-01
world: verse — the election, said over

for each cell
  the light arrives as a patch
  four wide six deep
  twenty-four small doors

for each glyph
  the atlas holds a shadow
  one number wide
  twenty-four small answers

the xor is the question
the popcount is the listening
the minimum is the choice

seventy candidates
seventy ways to be dark
the cell asks them all
and keeps the one
that rhymes

no float in the loop
no drift in the frame
only the count
of where the light
differs from the shape

and the grid holds
because the grid is held
cell by cell
bit by bit
a reading of the world
in twenty-fourths

amen is not the word
the word is the glyph
the glyph is the weight
the weight is the light

set it
and the eye
does the rest
